
# Privacy Policy — Cyparta Client

**Last Updated: October 14, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Cyparta ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Cyparta Client mobile application (the "App"). The App is designed to help Cyparta clients access information about their projects, track project progress, and stay informed about project-related updates.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, and how it is protected.

## 2. Who Can Use the App

Cyparta Client is intended for clients of Cyparta who have an account or authorized access to the company's project management services. The App is not intended for unauthorized users.

## 3. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following information:

- **Account Information:** Information used to identify and authenticate your account, such as your name, email address, username, and authentication token.
- **Project Information:** Details about projects associated with your account, including project names, descriptions, statuses, progress, milestones, and related updates.
- **Communication and Shared Content:** Messages, comments, attachments, or other information you submit through the App, if these features are available.
- **Device and Notification Information:** Push notification tokens and related technical information used to deliver project notifications, if notifications are enabled.
- **Local Storage:** Certain information may be stored temporarily on your device to support app functionality, remember your preferences, or improve your experience.

The information processed depends on the features available in your version of the App and your interactions with it.

## 4. Device Permissions

The App may request access to device features when required by its functionality.

- **Notifications:** To send project updates and relevant alerts, if enabled.
- **Camera and Photo Library:** Only if a feature requires you to capture or attach images or files.
- **Microphone:** Only if voice recording or audio communication features are available and used.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device settings. The App only uses permissions that are necessary for the features provided.

## 5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide access to projects associated with your account.
- Display project details, statuses, progress, and updates.
- Facilitate communication between clients and Cyparta, where supported.
- Send relevant project notifications, where enabled.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.
- Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized access.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use personal information for advertising, and we do not sell personal information.

## 6. Information Sharing

We may share information only when necessary:

- **Within Cyparta:** With authorized team members who need access to manage projects and provide services to clients.
- **With Service Providers:** With trusted providers that support the App's hosting, infrastructure, authentication, or notifications, where applicable.
- **For Legal Reasons:** When disclosure is required by applicable law or is necessary to protect our legal rights and security.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored on Cyparta's systems or through the service providers used to operate the App, as applicable.

We use HTTPS encryption for data transmitted between the App and supported servers and apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide our services, manage client projects, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Information stored locally on your device may remain available until it is removed by the App, you sign out, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App, depending on the type of information and how it is stored.

Deleting the App does not necessarily delete information held on Cyparta's servers.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and legitimate business or legal requirements, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

To submit a request or ask a question about your privacy, contact us using the details provided below.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Cyparta Client is intended for business clients and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be published with a revised "Last Updated" date.
